Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Short version. The Polkadex Explorer is a read-only window onto a public blockchain. We don't run analytics, we don't set tracking cookies, we don't sell or share data with third parties. We store a handful of small items in your browser's local storage to remember your preferences, and our server logs the kind of request data a normal web server logs. One exception to "nothing leaves this site": if you connect a phone wallet, your browser talks to the WalletConnect relay, run by Reown. It carries encrypted messages and sees your IP address. It is off unless you choose it. See section 4.

1. Who we are

The Polkadex Explorer (explorer.polkadex.ee) is operated by the Polkadex Foundation. This policy covers the explorer specifically; the Foundation's wider organisational privacy policy is available at the Polkadex Privacy Policy.

2. What we store about you

The explorer stores three kinds of data:

a) On-chain data. All blocks, extrinsics, events, balances, validators, governance proposals, and votes you see on this site are already public on the Polkadex Mainnet. We index them so you can browse them efficiently. Anyone with a Polkadex node has access to the same data. We cannot delete on-chain data — that's a property of the blockchain itself, not of this explorer.

b) Local storage on your device. We keep a small set of keys in your browser's localStorage so the explorer remembers your choices between visits. See the Cookie & Storage Notice for the full list. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly sign a transaction or post a community label.

c) Server logs. Our backend logs the standard request data a web server records: source IP (often a Cloudflare IP, since we're behind Cloudflare), user-agent, requested URL, response code, and timestamp. We use these to debug errors and measure traffic shape. Logs are retained for 30 days and then rotated out.

3. Wallet addresses and signatures

If you connect a wallet, the explorer reads your account's public address from it: from your browser extension, or from your phone wallet over WalletConnect. Public addresses are not personal data on their own under most privacy frameworks (they are pseudonymous identifiers anyone can derive from a public key), but they can become personal if linked to your identity, which is something you control. We never request your private key, mnemonic, or secret seed phrase. Any wallet extension that asks the explorer for these is malicious.

When you post a community label, a discussion message, or a vote, your wallet signs the action with your private key. The signed payload, including your address and the signature, is stored on our server (for labels and discussions) or broadcast to the chain (for votes). You can ask us to remove labels, votes, or discussion messages you authored; contact us via the channels listed below.

4. Third-party services

The explorer integrates with:

  • Cloudflare as a CDN and DDoS shield. Cloudflare sees your IP. Their privacy policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • Browser wallet extensions (Polkadot.js, Talisman, SubWallet, Nova Wallet) when you click Connect Wallet. The explorer doesn't share any data with them; they expose your account list to us with your explicit permission.
  • The WalletConnect relay (relay.walletconnect.org, run by Reown, Inc.), only if you connect a phone wallet from the Connect wallet page (the "Phone wallet" option; "Wallet app" on a phone). Your browser and your wallet app talk through this relay. The messages are end-to-end encrypted: the relay cannot read your accounts, your keys, or what you sign. It does see your IP address, that the connection comes from explorer.polkadex.ee, your browser and operating system, our project id, a random id your browser makes the first time and reuses on later connections, and the size and timing of the messages. When the explorer asks your wallet to sign, the relay also sees whether it is a transaction or a message and for which chain, and your wallet app may add the hash of the signed transaction, which matches it to that transaction once it is on chain. While your phone wallet stays connected, the explorer contacts the relay each time you open a page, so the connection keeps working. Choose "End connection" or "Disconnect" and it stops. If you never use the phone option, no WalletConnect code is loaded and your browser never contacts the relay. We have switched off the library's usage reporting to Reown, and our Content-Security-Policy blocks its other services.
  • A PDEX price feed (CoinGecko/Coinpaprika public API) that runs server-side. Your browser does not contact these endpoints directly.
  • A transactional email provider (Postmark by default; SendGrid as an alternative) that delivers your governance email alerts if and only if you opt in via the "Get email alerts" button. They see your email address and the alert content to send the message; they don't get your IP, your wallet, or anything else from us. We don't share your address with them for any purpose besides delivery.

We do not load Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, advertising pixels, or any other analytics/tracking script. Every script, stylesheet, and font is self-hosted from explorer.polkadex.ee, including the icon font and the Inter typeface, which we serve ourselves rather than from Google Fonts, so viewing this site sends no request to Google or to any CDN other than Cloudflare in front of us. A Content-Security-Policy header enforces this: the browser itself refuses to load scripts from anywhere else. The same policy lets your browser open a connection to exactly two other hosts: rpc.polkadex.ee (our own node, for live blocks and for sending what you sign) and relay.walletconnect.org (only if you connect a phone wallet, as described above).

5. Email alerts (optional)

If you opt in to email alerts via the "Get email alerts" button, we store your email address, a randomly-generated confirmation token, an independent randomly-generated unsubscribe token, your selected event preferences, and timestamps. We use the email solely to deliver the alerts you subscribed to. There is no marketing.

Subscriptions use double opt-in: we email a confirmation link first, and only confirmed subscribers ever receive alerts. Every alert email contains a one-click unsubscribe link that works without login. Unsubscribing immediately stops all future alerts. We retain your subscription row marked as unsubscribed (rather than deleting) so accidental re-signups can be re-confirmed by you — request full deletion via the contact channels below and we'll wipe the row.

Validator alerts. If the explorer offers the "Get alerts for this validator" control on a validator status page and you use it, we store your email address, the validator stash address you chose, a confirmation token and an independent unsubscribe token for that validator, timestamps, and a log of which alerts were sent to you. We email you only about that validator, and only when it is not elected for a new era, has sent no heartbeat and authored no block by 85% of a session, is disabled, is slashed, changes its commission, or changes its session keys, one email for each occurrence. The same double opt-in applies: nothing is sent about a validator until you use the confirmation link, which works for 24 hours. Every alert has an unsubscribe link that works without login and ends only that validator; you can follow at most five validators with one address. Unsubscribing stops the email at once; we keep the row marked as unsubscribed, and you can ask us to delete it through the contact channels below. The address is not shared and is used for nothing else. The same email provider delivers these messages, and the control is off unless the operator has switched validator alerts on.

6. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to (a) know what personal data we hold about you, (b) request a copy, (c) request correction or deletion, and (d) object to processing. For the explorer specifically:

— Local storage: clear it any time via your browser settings, or click "Reset preferences" on the Cookies page.

— Community labels, votes, discussion messages: message us with the wallet that signed them (we'll verify via a fresh signature) and we'll delete them.

— Server access logs: we don't have a way to identify you within them unless you tell us your IP and time-window, but if you do, we'll delete matching entries on request.

— On-chain data: we cannot delete this. It's on the blockchain, not on our server.

7. Children

The explorer isn't directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly process data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has used the explorer in a way that needs attention, contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when this policy changes. Material changes will also be announced on our community channels.

9. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/Polkadex-Substrate or reach the team via the official channels listed at polkadex.ee.

Cookie & Storage Notice

Last updated: November 2026

The Polkadex Explorer does not set tracking cookies. We use your browser's localStorage to remember your preferences between visits. Local storage stays on your device and is never sent over the network to us — unlike cookies, which are sent with every request.

What we store

Every key the explorer writes is prefixed with pdex_ for easy identification. Here's the full list:

Key What it stores Why
pdex_wallet_addressConnected wallet address.So the My Account page works without re-connecting on every reload.
pdex_multisigs_v1Saved multisig names, public signer addresses, and approval thresholds.So imported multisigs remain available in My Accounts. Kept until you clear site storage; no private keys are stored.
pdex_multisig_call:*Reviewed call data and runtime version, keyed by network, multisig address, and call hash.To review and complete pending multisig calls on this browser. Kept until you clear site storage.
pdex_last_used_wallet_addressThe most recently active address when your wallet has multiple accounts.So we can default to the right account instead of always picking the first one.
pdex_wallet_sourceWhich wallet extension (for example polkadot-js, talisman or subwallet-js) the connected account was chosen from.So a signing request goes to the extension you picked when the same account exists in more than one.
pdex_wallet_last_sourceThe wallet (for example polkadot-js or talisman) you last connected an account from. Removed when you click Disconnect.So we only ever ask that wallet without a click, and never show another wallet's permission popup you did not ask for.
pdex_wallet_disconnectedA flag set when you click Disconnect and cleared when you connect an account.So we do not reconnect an account for you on the next visit; you see the account picker instead.
pdex_wallet_session_revA number that goes up each time the connected account, its wallet extension or a Disconnect changes, in any tab.So a signing request started before such a change is refused instead of being sent, even if you switch back to the same account.
pdex_validate_enabledA flag set when you open the explorer with ?validate=1 and removed with ?validate=0. Only used while the Validate page is being tested.So this browser shows the Validate page (validator status and the session keys checker) before it is switched on for everyone.
pdex_wc_enabledSet to 0 if you open the explorer with ?wc=0 (1 with ?wc=1). Absent for most visitors.So this browser remembers that you switched the Phone wallet option (WalletConnect) off.
pdex_wc_debugA flag set when you open the explorer with ?wcdebug=1 and removed with ?wcdebug=0. Only used to diagnose WalletConnect signing problems.So a failed phone-wallet signature shows support details (the transaction and signature, nothing secret) that you can copy and send to us.
pdex_wc_sessionA flag set when you connect a phone wallet with WalletConnect, removed when the connection ends.So the explorer reconnects to your phone wallet when you come back. Without it no WalletConnect code is loaded.
wc@2:*Written by the WalletConnect library, in IndexedDB or local storage, only after you choose the Phone wallet option: the encrypted pairing and session with your phone wallet and its keys for the relay. No private key of your account; that stays in your wallet.So the connection to your phone wallet survives a reload. The library may keep some entries after the connection ends; clearing this site's data in your browser removes them ("Reset preferences" clears only the pdex_ keys).
pdex_watchlist_v1Addresses, validators, and governance items you've starred.So your watchlist follows you across sessions.
pdex_discuss_sessionSigned bearer tokens for posting on the discussion board, one per wallet account you have posted with, each with its expiry (7 days, matching the server-side session TTL). These are credentials - anyone with one can post as that address until it expires. Disconnect or Reset clears them and signs them out on the server.So you don't have to re-sign for every post, or after switching accounts.
pdex_tour_seen_v1A boolean flag once you've completed the onboarding tour.So we don't pop the tour up again every visit.
pdex_banner_dismissedA flag set when you dismiss the storage notice banner.So we don't show it again.
pdex_stake_filters_v1The filters, past return window and sort order you last used in the Stake PDEX validator picker (no addresses, no amounts).So the picker opens the way you left it.
pdex_price_periodThe time window you last picked on the full-screen /price chart (7 / 30 / 90 / 365 / 4000 days).So your selection persists between visits.
pdex_home_snapshotA small JSON cache of the home-page stat values (price, market cap, total issuance, in-stake, AVG APY, era, validators, nominators, max active stake) plus a save timestamp.So returning visitors see populated home-page cells instantly while live data loads in the background. Expires after 7 days.
pdex_wallet_dashboard:<address>One JSON snapshot of the My Account dashboard payload (balance, staking positions, recent activity, price history, reward history) per address you've viewed, plus a save timestamp.So returning to the dashboard renders instantly while the live data refreshes in the background. Expires after 30 minutes.
pdex_known_resultsFor a rewards payout you sent in the last 10 minutes: which reward entries (era and validator) it paid or is still checking, with your account address, the block number and the transaction hash.So a reward that is already paid is not offered to you again, after a reload or in another tab, while the explorer catches up with the chain. Expires after 10 minutes.
pdex_gov_seen_refThe highest democracy referendum index you've been notified of.So new-event banners and toasts only pop for referenda tabled after the last one you saw.
pdex_gov_seen_proposalThe highest public-proposal index you've been notified of.Same as above for democracy public proposals.
pdex_gov_banner_dismissed_refIndex of the most recent referendum banner you closed.So a banner you dismissed stays dismissed for that specific event; a newer event still triggers a fresh one.
pdex_gov_banner_dismissed_proposalIndex of the most recent proposal banner you closed.Same as above for public proposals.
Email alerts (if you opted in) are NOT stored in your browser. Your email address and preferences live on our server only. See the privacy policy section 5 for details.

What we do NOT store

We do not set any tracking cookies. We do not load Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, advertising pixels, or any other analytics or tracking script. Every script runs from explorer.polkadex.ee; none is loaded from another site. The open-source libraries we bundle are listed under Licenses. One of them, WalletConnect, contacts an outside server, and only if you connect a phone wallet. See the Privacy Policy, section 4.

Sessions

We do not use sessionStorage for wallet state. Whether you are connected, and whether you chose to disconnect, is remembered in the localStorage keys above until you switch or disconnect again.

Reset everything

You can clear every pdex_* key in your browser at any time. The button below removes them all and reloads the page.

Equivalent to clearing site data in your browser's developer tools.
